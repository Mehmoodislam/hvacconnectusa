Our Story

Born from a Passion for Honest, Rapid Home Service

When an air conditioner dies during a 105-degree July heatwave, or a furnace goes silent on a sub-zero Christmas Eve, homeowners don’t have days to wait for a service call. Yet for decades, traditional contractor companies responded with voicemails, vague arrival windows, and surprise holiday surge bills.

HVACConnectUSA was founded in 2010 to revolutionize emergency home services. We built a real-time smart dispatching network that links vetted, licensed master technicians directly to households in need. With fully equipped service vans carrying over 200 OEM components, our specialists resolve over 92% of cooling, heating, and plumbing emergencies on the very first visit.
